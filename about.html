ABOUT CAREER TOOLS

Practical tools for the small decisions behind a job search

Career Tools is a browser-first collection of free utilities for resumes, job applications, interviews, compensation comparisons and career planning.

Why this site exists

A job search is made up of many individual tasks. A person may need to compare a resume with a job description, rewrite a weak bullet, prepare a behavioral story, estimate compensation or plan a career transition. These tasks are often scattered across different websites. Career Tools brings them into one focused toolkit.

Designed around useful tasks

Each tool is intended to solve a specific problem instead of acting as a generic content page. The site combines practical calculators, text analysis, structured generators and planning checklists. The goal is to make each result understandable enough that users can review it and decide what to do next.

Browser-first by design

Many tools process text and calculations directly in the browser. This makes the interaction fast and reduces the need to send ordinary tool inputs to a server. Users should still avoid entering sensitive information unless it is necessary for the task.

Results are assistance, not guarantees

A resume score is not a hiring probability. A salary calculation is not financial advice. A generated interview answer is not a substitute for knowing your own experience. Career Tools provides structured assistance and transparent estimates so users can make their own decisions.

How the site is maintained

The toolkit is continuously reviewed for usability, input validation, mobile layout, navigation and content quality. New guides are added when they can provide useful context around the tools rather than simply creating pages for search engines.

Start with a task

Choose one problem you are solving today, use the relevant tool, review the result and then personalize the final decision or document.
