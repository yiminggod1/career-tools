CONTACT & FEEDBACK

Help improve Career Tools

Useful feedback helps us find broken calculations, confusing instructions, missing career topics and mobile usability problems.

What to report

  • A tool that produces an unexpected or clearly incorrect result.
  • A form field or button that does not work.
  • A page that looks broken on a phone or desktop browser.
  • A typo, outdated explanation or unclear instruction.
  • A career task that would benefit from a focused tool or guide.

When reporting a problem

Please describe the tool name, the browser or device you used, the input pattern that caused the issue and what you expected to happen. Do not include passwords, private account information, full resumes containing sensitive personal data or other information that does not need to be shared.

General information

Career Tools is a free utility website. Tool results are informational and should be reviewed before being used in applications, negotiations or other important decisions.
