WHY USE CAREER TOOLS?

From resume keywords to interview preparation

Job searching has many small decisions: whether a resume is readable, which skills a job description emphasizes, how to structure a STAR story, or how an offer changes your annual compensation. Career Tools puts those tasks into focused pages so you can solve one problem at a time.

Are the tools free?

Yes. The core tools are free and designed without an account.

Do I need to upload a resume?

No. Most text tools accept pasted text and process it in the browser.

Are results guaranteed to improve hiring outcomes?

No. These tools are practical aids, not guarantees. Review results and personalize your application.
