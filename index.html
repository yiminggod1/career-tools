WHY CAREER TOOLS?

One workspace for the messy middle of a job search.

Job searching is rarely one big problem. It is a chain of smaller decisions: whether a resume is readable, which skills a role emphasizes, how to structure a STAR story, or how an offer changes your real annual compensation. Career Tools keeps those decisions in focused, practical pages.

Are the tools free?

Yes. The core tools are free and designed without an account.

Do I need to upload a resume?

No. Most text tools accept pasted text and process it in the browser.

Are results guaranteed to improve hiring outcomes?

No. These tools are practical aids, not guarantees. Review results and personalize your application.
