WHY THIS SITE

A utility desk, not a content maze.

Career decisions are made in small steps. Check a resume. Understand a job description. Practice an answer. Compare an offer. Career Tools keeps those jobs separate, fast and easy to revisit.

Are the tools free?

Yes. The core tools are free and designed without an account.

Do I need to upload a resume?

No. Most text tools accept pasted text and process it in the browser.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Results are practical aids. Review them and personalize your final application materials.
