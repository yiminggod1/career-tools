About Salary Increase Calculator

A salary change is easier to understand when you can see both the dollar difference and the percentage change.

How to use it well

Enter the old and new salaries and use both figures when discussing compensation changes.

What to review

  • Adapt the output to the actual role or situation.
  • Review important assumptions before using the result.

Common mistake

A percentage change does not capture benefits, bonus structure, taxes or changes in responsibilities.
