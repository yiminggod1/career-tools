About Skills Gap Checker

A skills gap is most useful when it is tied to a specific target role rather than a generic list of desirable skills.

How to use it well

Compare your current capabilities with the requirements of one target role, then prioritize gaps by importance and realistic learning effort.

What to review

  • Keep inputs and statements accurate.
  • Check assumptions before acting on the result.
  • Adapt the final output to the specific role or situation.

Common mistake

Not every missing skill needs immediate action. Focus on gaps that materially affect the roles you want.
