About Resume vs Job Matcher

Matching is most useful when it highlights where your real evidence aligns with a target role and where it is missing.

How to use it well

Compare the resume with one specific job description, review the strongest overlaps and gaps, then decide which gaps are genuine and which are simply wording differences.

What to review

  • Use accurate, role-specific information.
  • Check assumptions before relying on a calculation or suggestion.
  • Personalize the final result for your situation.

Common mistake

Do not rewrite your history just to increase a match. Use accurate evidence and add relevant detail only when it is true.
