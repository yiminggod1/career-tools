About Behavioral Interview Questions

Behavioral questions are designed to surface examples of how you handled real situations, decisions and outcomes.

How to use it well

Practice with examples from different projects and make your own actions and results clear.

What to review

  • Adapt the output to the actual role or situation.
  • Review important assumptions before using the result.

Common mistake
